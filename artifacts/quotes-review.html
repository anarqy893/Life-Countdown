A first collection for Life Countdown: finite time, attention, meaningful choices, relationships, rest, and appreciation. The original selections are short excerpts checked against the linked texts, not newly written paraphrases. Later additions identify any uncertain attribution. Initial capitals, punctuation, and line breaks are normalized for display; an ellipsis marks an internal omission. Older translations sometimes use formal language. Fictional dialogue is identified. This is a review collection; it has not been added to the app.
